# Mission

**Build your own hev mind.**

This is the mission of the `kit` line (the KIT team in Linear) and of its
repos: hev/kit, the open kit, and hev/kit-pro. Two clauses, and each is a
test a plan has to pass.

## Your own

hev mind runs on memory: every session its agents ran, kept, searchable and
read before the next one starts. kit is that memory, packaged so anyone can
have it on their own machines, free and local by default, with no account
and no key. A developer with one laptop and a team with a fleet should get
the same thing, and their transcripts never leave their control unless they
choose a hosted lane. The measures are the time from `brew install` to a
first search over your own traces, and how much of it runs with nothing
hosted at all.

## A hev mind

Searchable traces are the floor, not the goal. The goal is what hev mind
does with them: agents that know what was tried before, sessions graded and
improved from their own records, and an operator who can ask what happened
instead of reading transcripts. A plan earns its place by moving a kit user
closer to that, and kit is how hev mind itself runs, so every feature has a
real user on day one.

## What this rules in

- Anything that shortens the path from install to useful memory.
- Anything hev mind does with its traces that a kit user can't do yet.
- Retrieval needs filed on LYR and met through Layer.

## What this rules out

- Retrieval built inside kit instead of on Layer.
- Features hev mind's own fleet doesn't use.
- Anything that makes a hosted service necessary to run kit.

## How the machine reads this

The coordinating model reads this file when it plans work on this line, and
judges what it is about to start, and what is already in flight, against a
named clause. RFCs on the KIT team are drafted against it.
