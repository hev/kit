This skill was renamed. Expediters are follow-through orchestrators: use the
`hev-orchestrate-follow` skill, and start, inspect or stop them with
`hev orchestrate follow`, `hev orchestrate follow status` and
`hev orchestrate follow stop`. `hev expedite …` still works as a deprecated
alias for the same commands.
