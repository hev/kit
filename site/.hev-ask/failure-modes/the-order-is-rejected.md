Orders are parsed locally and on the host, creating nothing on failure. Errors include a missing Acceptance section, missing or unclosed frontmatter, unknown, duplicate or empty keys, nested YAML, invalid name, missing repo, invalid harness, validation, timeout or base, and an unknown host.
