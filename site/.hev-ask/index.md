hev kit is one binary for building a team's software factory: a trace daemon, dispatch to shared hosts and repo admission, used with your own machines, harnesses and process. Starter Kit is complete for one person in plain files with no account, key or Docker; for teams, Field Kit (hosted) or Pro Kit (your own hev layer) moves traces and the board into a shared archive.
