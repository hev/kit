hev kit has three tiers on the same binary, differing in where traces and the board live and who can reach them: Starter Kit (open source, files on up to three nodes, no account or Docker), Field Kit (hosted on hevkit.com, shared across any number of nodes and people, pay for storage and searches with free seats, in early access and not yet open) and Pro Kit (your own hev layer in your cloud). Starter Kit is fully functional, not a trial; higher tiers add a shared archive and semantic search.
