hev kit has three tiers that run the same binary and differ in where traces and the board are kept and who can reach them. Starter Kit is open source under Apache-2.0, runs on up to three of your own nodes in plain files with no account, key or Docker; Field Kit is hosted on hevkit.com, shares traces, the board and peers across any number of nodes and people, charges for storage and searches with free seats, and is in early access and not yet open; Pro Kit is bring-your-own-cloud, running hev layer against your own store. Starter Kit is not a trial: capture, search, dashboard, cost, board, dispatch, validation and orchestrators all work, and the other tiers add one shared archive and search by meaning.
