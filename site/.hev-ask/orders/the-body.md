Write the body for a capable engineer with only the repo and no history: what is wrong with evidence, where to look, the decision already made, constraints, where to stop and what to report rather than change. Without a stop instruction the worker commits, pushes, opens a draft PR and writes a report; skip step-by-step instructions the worker can infer from the code.
