Every launch, including rework, retry, messages to ended runs and restarting amendments, builds a fresh bundle and digest kept in the run record (never the worktree). Run metadata records the recipe, its hash and commit, the digest and sources; results and worker session rows also carry the digest. Bundles are not sent anywhere new.
