Owner versus member: owners have shell access and control everything (software, credentials, admission, members, all runs, attach, rework, validation, orchestrators), while members have a key locked to the dispatch RPC and can only send and manage their own runs, with every run stamped with their name. The repos permission is the only extra right today. The member boundary does not limit what agents do once running as the host account, so invite only trusted people and keep the account narrow.
