Overview of hev kit's two kinds of HTTP API: local APIs served on your own machines on every tier (the trace dashboard and the board), and the hosted Field Kit API, which is in early access and not yet deployed. The CLI is a client of these APIs; they exist for your own scripts, dashboards and tools.
