Acceptance for the ship role (a draft PR from the chosen branch with the verdict linked), followed by how to dry-run, start, inspect and take down the Gauntlet swarm. The compiled orchestrator is scoped to the swarm's own runs with a three-day lifetime; crashed or stalled builders are restarted in place a limited number of times, first-green only counts a passing verdict, and a lifetime cap on attempts is planned for a later phase.
