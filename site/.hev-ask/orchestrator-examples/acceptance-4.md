Acceptance for the swarm's ship role (a draft PR from the chosen branch with the verdict linked), plus how to dry-run, start, inspect, tail logs and tear down the proposed swarm, which only exists on an unmerged draft branch. The swarm compiles into a scoped orchestrator; crashed or stalled builders restart in place a limited number of times, and until a lifetime attempt cap ships the stop is the daily run budget and max lifetime.
