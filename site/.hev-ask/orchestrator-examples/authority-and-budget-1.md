The release coordinator can rework or retry red or dead runs, start pending validation, dispatch a notes fix limited by issue scope, message workers and notify you. Its daily run budget counts every start or restart and its concurrent cap protects other work; admission enforces the budget while the one-rework rule is just guidance.
