The release coordinator gets rework and retry to send back or restart a run once, validate for pending validations, dispatch for the release-notes fix-up, and say and notify for communication. Admission restricts it to orders on the train in one repo and enforces the daily and concurrency budget, while the one-rework rule is only a guideline to the model.
