Why the release coordinator gets each authority: rework and retry for red or dead runs, validate for pending browser validation, dispatch for the release-notes fix-up, say to inform workers and notify to reach you. Admission limits it to the train's issue prefix and repo; the daily run budget counts every start including reworks and validations, while the one-rework rule is only guidance to the model.
