The Gauntlet loop as a swarm: a build role races two builders on different harnesses and models, each checked by the same independent browser validator, and the first to finish with a pushed head and passing verdict wins. A ship role then opens one draft PR from the winner; there is no critic role because kit's critic is the validator.
