How kit maps the Gauntlet loop: frozen bar is the order's Acceptance plus a pinned reference commit; builder is the dispatched run; blind critic is browser validation on another host with a different harness/model; the gate is the verdict needing evidence; failing the same item twice makes needs-review and refuses verdict-based rework.
