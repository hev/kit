Maps Gauntlet concepts onto kit: the frozen bar is the order's acceptance plus a commit-pinned reference, the builder is the dispatched run, the blind critic is browser validation on another host with a different harness and model, the gate is the verdict, and the loop is rework from validation. The same item failing twice sends the run to needs-review, and unlike Shumer's critic the validator is blind to the builder rather than to which artifact is which.
