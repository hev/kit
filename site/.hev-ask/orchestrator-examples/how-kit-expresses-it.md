Maps Gauntlet loop concepts to kit: the frozen bar is the order's Acceptance plus a pinned reference, the builder is the dispatched run, the blind critic is browser validation on another host with a different harness and model, the gate is the verdict (two failures of the same item make the run needs-review), the loop is rework from validation, and the stop is the goal plus budget and lifetime. Unlike Shumer's version, kit's validator is blind to the builder, not to which artifact is the reference.
