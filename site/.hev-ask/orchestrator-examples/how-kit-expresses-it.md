Maps the Gauntlet loop onto kit: the frozen bar is the order's Acceptance plus a pinned reference, the builder is the dispatched run, the blind critic is browser validation on another host and model, the gate is the verdict, the loop is rework from validation, and the stop is the goal plus daily runs and lifetime. kit's validator is blind to the builder, not to which artifact is which.
