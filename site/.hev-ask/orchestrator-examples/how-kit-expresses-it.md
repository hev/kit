Maps Gauntlet concepts to kit: the frozen bar is the order's Acceptance plus a pinned reference commit, the builder is the dispatched run, the blind critic is browser validation on another host with a different harness and model, the gate is the verdict, and the loop is rework from validation. One difference is that the validator is blind to the builder but not to which artifact is which.
