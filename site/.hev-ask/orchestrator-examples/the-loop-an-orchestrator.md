The Gauntlet orchestrator spec loops until its goal is met within a three-day lifetime, managing one named run. Its brief says it runs the loop and never judges the work: restart pending validation, rework from the verdict on a fail, report the head on a full pass, stop and escalate on needs-review, and never alter the acceptance.
