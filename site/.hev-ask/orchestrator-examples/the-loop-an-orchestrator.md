The Gauntlet orchestrator spec: loop mode every 15m until goal with 3d lifetime, claude harness, managing one named run, authority say/notify/rework/validate and tight budget. The brief says it runs the loop but doesn't judge; the validator's verdict is the only judgement, and failed verdicts trigger rework from validation.
