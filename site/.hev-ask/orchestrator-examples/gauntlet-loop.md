The Gauntlet loop is a technique by Matt Shumer from July 2026: a builder works to a fixed bar, a separate critic with fresh context judges against a reference, and work returns to the builder until it wins. The stop is left open in the original, whereas in kit the stop is in the spec and admission enforces it.
