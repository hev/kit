Three copyable orchestrator specs: an intake loop that turns tracker issues into orders on a schedule, a release coordinator that keeps a release train moving, and a Gauntlet loop that reworks one piece of work until an independent critic passes it. Each shows the spec, commands, a round, authority and budget, stop conditions and what it asks a person; none can merge, because kit grants no merge authority.
