Amending replaces a run's whole order while keeping name, branch and PR, saving history and diffs. Body edits reach a live worker through its inbox, some fields update in place, changing host, harness, model or base restarts the attempt, and host moves check capacity and admission and carry work, inbox and history to the new host.
