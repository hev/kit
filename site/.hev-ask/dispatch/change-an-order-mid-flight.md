Amending replaces a run's whole order while keeping its name, repo, branch and PR, saving history and diffs. On a live run, body edits go to the worker's inbox and some settings update in place, while changing host, harness, model or base restarts the attempt; host moves check capacity and admission and carry work, inbox and history along.
