Troubleshooting for members: a missing Tailscale machine means the invite wasn't accepted or used another account; expired codes need a new one; already-joined means check your node list; public key denials mean Adam hasn't accepted yet; revoked access means ask Adam; repo refusals mean the repo isn't admitted. To leave, tell Adam and remove the local key, SSH config block and dispatch entry.
