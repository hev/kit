Lists the swarm commands: dry-run or apply a file, list swarms and units, read supervisor, round or unit logs, message the orchestrator or one unit, pick a race winner, pause, resume and take down. Units are addressed by role and piece or variant; taking a swarm down keeps branches, worktrees and records, role changes apply in place on a running swarm, and orchestrator field changes need a down then up.
