License states: licensed (valid key, all Pro), grace (up to 14 days after a paid license expires, with warnings; trials skip it) and floor (no key, invalid, other product, or after grace: Starter Kit behavior). Floor only refuses new Pro-requiring operations; existing data and workspaces keep working.
