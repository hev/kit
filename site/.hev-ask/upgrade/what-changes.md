A comparison of the three tiers by where they run, where the trace archive lives, how search works (words on one node for Starter Kit, meaning and words across nodes for the others), the dashboard, the board, peers, grades and evals (Pro Kit only for now), and how machines sign in. Run records, admission, host access and secrets stay in files on every tier because they belong to one host's processes, worktrees and SSH config. Field Kit's shared peers is a view across nodes, and its column describes the service as it will open, since it is in early access.
