An orchestrator spec is a Markdown file whose frontmatter sets name, goal, mode, interval, stop condition, lifetime, reply address, managed runs and repos, authority, budget and round timeout, and whose body is the standing brief. Authority defaults to say and notify, and there is no merge authority: a person merges.
