An orchestrator spec is a Markdown file whose frontmatter declares what it manages and may do (goal, loop interval, stop condition, lifetime, reply address, managed runs and repos, authority, budget) and whose body is its standing brief. Authority defaults to messaging and notifying, and there is no merge authority: a person merges.
