An orchestrator spec is a Markdown file whose frontmatter sets name, goal, loop or once mode, cadence, stop condition, lifetime, reply address, managed runs and repos, authority verbs, budget and round timeout, with the body as its standing brief. Authority defaults to messaging and notifying, and there is no merge authority.
