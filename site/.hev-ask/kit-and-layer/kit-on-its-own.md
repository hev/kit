Without a key, setup installs the daemon and skills without Docker, and sessions are scrubbed and written to a local archive with a word index, relocatable by config or environment. Search matches words only, so pair natural phrasing with exact tokens; the archive grows without pruning (about 400 MB per thousand sessions) and holds only that machine's sessions.
