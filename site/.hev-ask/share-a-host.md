Sharing one host across a team gives the most value: the host holds the repos, bot identity and credentials, tools, skills and trace archive, while each developer sends work from a laptop that shares nothing with it. A host can be a physical machine or a VM, each with its own owner, members and admission list, and the bot account should be scoped like a new hire with a human approving its work.
