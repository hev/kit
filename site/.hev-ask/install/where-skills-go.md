Skills are installed only in your home folder, never system-wide: per-harness skill folders, a shared folder read by Codex, pi and OpenCode, pinned copies of team skills, a local bin for commands that come with team skills, and a state folder that records what was installed. Your own skills and local edits are never overwritten unless you force it, and check and sync commands report and repair missing or stale copies; you can also point trace capture at an existing hev layer archive.
