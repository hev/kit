The dashboard's Asks view lists traces newest first with root ask, hosts, orders, runs, state, cost and duration; opening one shows a chronological waterfall grouped by ask, with follow-up asks labelled by type, relaunched orders marked as continuations, validations with verdicts, and session rows that open into turns and tool calls. You can filter to a single order.
