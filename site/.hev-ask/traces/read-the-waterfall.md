The dashboard's Asks view lists traces newest first with root ask text, hosts, orders, runs, state, cost and duration, and opening one shows a waterfall grouped by ask. Follow-up asks are labelled continue, rework, retry, say, amend or validate, with the orders, attempts, validation verdicts and sessions under them, and sessions open to turns, tool calls and the full transcript view.
