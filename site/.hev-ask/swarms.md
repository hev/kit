A swarm is one Markdown file declaring a problem, an orchestrator and worker roles with concurrency limits, compiled into an ordinary orchestrator and dispatch runs. A code-only reconcile tick launches units, repairs failures in place and starts integration, and a person merges; in phase 1 every unit runs on the host where the swarm is started, with multi-host placement, scaling and more in later phases.
