A member cannot add a third-party repo outside the allowed organization prefixes; they either fork it into one of your organizations or ask the owner to add that one repo on the host. Do not widen the prefixes to a whole hosting site, and grant the right only to people you would let choose the bot's work, since the list controls where agents run, not what code they can read.
