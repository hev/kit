Members cannot add third-party repos (such as open-source libraries) outside the allowed organizations; they can fork into an allowed organization or ask the owner to add it. Avoid broad prefixes like all of GitHub. The list only limits which repos agents run in, not what code they can read, and the grant lets a member make the host's bot clone anything its credentials reach under the allowed prefixes.
