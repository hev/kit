The app icon is one square PNG of at least 512 pixels kept beside the config; the app derives home-screen, large, maskable and Apple touch sizes named by the original file's hash so phones do not keep stale icons. A safe-zone heuristic warns when artwork falls outside the central circle, iOS usually needs the app re-added after an icon change, and the app reads and acts on runs, decisions, hosts, peers, the board and traces through the hev binary.
