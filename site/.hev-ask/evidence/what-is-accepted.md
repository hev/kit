Accepted types are decided by file bytes: images (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, 10 MiB), video (MP4, MOV, WebM, 100 MiB and 60s), and UTF-8 text (5 MiB). SVG, BMP, TIFF, binary and corrupt files are refused. Packages cap at 64 items, 32 claims, 20 tags; a refused file writes nothing.
