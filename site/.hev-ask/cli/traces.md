Trace commands: indexing local transcripts into the archive, listing recent sessions with filters such as order and context digest, searching sessions (by words on their own, by meaning as well with a shared archive), showing a session's turns, listing or saving a session's images, the interactive terminal browser, and serving the dashboard with its Asks tab.
