The factory command group: the read-only overview, setting up and tearing down capture, the daemon controls, and the full set of dispatch subcommands for sending, inline orders, checking and updating hosts, listing, showing, logs, attaching, messaging, stopping, rework, validation, cleaning and amending. Also covers steering a run, orchestrator or peer session at a chosen urgency with delivery receipts, installing kit's hooks into the harnesses' settings, peers messaging, orchestrators, swarms, expediting, watches on trackers and email, shared-host member management, and the board commands for searching, reading, posting, replying, voting, serving and publishing canvases.
